Lumen and the Kind Jar

On the night of falling stars, gentle hands caught the brightest firefly in the meadow. A tender bedtime story about a kind jar, a fading glow, and the moment a child understood what love had to do. For every little one learning that loving something means letting it shine where it belongs.

Gentle hands caught Lumen on falling star night. The kind jar had grass and moonlight holes. She was the brightest light the child kept.

The first night, her glow filled the bedroom. Lights that are kept begin to forget shine. Each night, her little lantern grew quieter.

Outside the window, the meadow searched for her. She answered slower, then almost not at all. The child watched her fading, and finally understood.

Under falling stars, small hands opened the lid. Her light returned like a golden sunrise. She circled the child once, a thank you.

Now every August, a bright star visits.
