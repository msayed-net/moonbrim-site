The Loaf That Rose Slowly

In the warm night bakery, every quick roll puffed up before midnight — except Bun, who rose slowly, breath by tiny breath. A cozy bedtime story about ovens that glow like sunsets, flour that falls like snow, and why the festival bread is always the one that took its time. For every little one growing at their own pace.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Every night, the bakery whispered dough to sleep. Quick rolls puffed up proud before midnight. Bun rose slowly, one breath at a time.

One by one, others left for the fire. The oven glowed for everyone except him. The baker gently cupped him, patient and kind.

All through the dark hours, Bun kept growing. By the smallest hours, something wonderful was happening. At dawn, the baker lifted the cloth.

Then at last, the fire was all his. The festival bread that year was the finest. The new dough asked how he grew grand.

Slowly, the best things happen slowly.
