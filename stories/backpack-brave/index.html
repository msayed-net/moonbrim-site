The Backpack Who Was Scared of School

The night before the first day of school, Mila finds her brand new backpack hiding under the bed — trembling. A gentle bedtime story about first-day fear, and how comforting someone else is secretly how we get brave ourselves. For little dreamers starting something new, and the grown-ups walking them there.

The night before school, something hid nearby. Mila peeked under the bed, curious. Its zipper chattered like nervous teeth.

Tomorrow was the first day, not ready. So Mila packed it with brave treasures. They practiced being brave in the mirror first.

They practiced walking together to the door. But at the door, backpack ran back inside. Then Mila whispered that she was scared too.

The little backpack held her all night. Morning tiptoed in, and found them brave together. By morning, someone brave waited at the door.

And they walked in together, carrying each other.
