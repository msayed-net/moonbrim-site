The Hedgehog Who Threw a Star Back

The night a star fell from the sky, only one small hedgehog was brave enough to help. A cozy bedtime story about Juniper and the great silver web — perfect for the Perseid meteor shower season. New bedtime stories for kids and families.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Only Juniper was not afraid that night. A berry bounced off a silver web. She found the star cold and gray.

A star that sees sunrise never shines again. She threw the star from the tallest pine. It fell back down as the sky paled.

Juniper remembered the web that bounced her berry. She rolled the star onto the web. The star soared home before golden dawn.

That August a thousand stars fell down.
