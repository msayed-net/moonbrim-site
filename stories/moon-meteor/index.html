The Moon Who Went Dark

Every night, Luna lights the whole valley alone, until the night of the meteor shower, when nobody looks up at her at all. A gentle bedtime story about the Moon who dimmed herself dark on purpose, and the small wish that needed the darkness to come true. For every little one learning that some gifts are quiet ones.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Luna shone bright on the meteor night. Every night, Luna lit the whole valley alone. Nobody looks up at me on meteor nights.

She almost blazed full to outshine them all. A small child wished on a falling star. Luna understood what the small wish needed most.

She dimmed her light and watched them shine. The sky exploded into its greatest show yet. The whole valley gasped up at the stars.

A few meteors circled back to thank her. Slowly, the falling stars grew fewer and fewer. At dawn, the valley whispered thanks to darkness.

Luna returned bright, having taught the sky kindness.
