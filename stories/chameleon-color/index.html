Pixel and the Vanishing Trick

Pixel the chameleon can match anything, frogs, branches, stones, everything except herself. A gentle rainforest bedtime story about the day she faded almost invisible, and the rainstorm that washed her back into her one true color. For every little one learning it is safe to be different.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Pixel could be any color except her own. With the frogs, she wore their leafy green. With branches and stones, she wore their colors.

Slowly, her own colors began wearing thin too. She matched so well she began to vanish. Alone and clear, she waited for the rain.

The rain washed everything away except herself. Underneath, her true colors bloomed back to life. The whole rainforest went quiet, just to look.

For the first time, Pixel stayed exactly herself. One brave friend let its true color show. One by one, the whole forest turned vivid.

One true color started a forest of them.
