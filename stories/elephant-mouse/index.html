The Elephant Who Was Afraid of Small Things

The biggest baby at the river feared the smallest things. A gentle bedtime story about Juno, who could carry logs but jumped at beetles — and the dry season when watching the ground where small things live saved the whole herd. For every big kid with big feelings.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

The biggest baby feared the smallest things. Juno could carry logs but feared beetles. The herd laughed gently at her fear.

So she hid her fear like a shadow. One night, a cricket sang beneath her feet. Juno trumpeted and ran into the night.

After that, she walked alone at the back. Then the long dry season came. The herd searched but found only dust.

But Juno watched the ground closely. A thin silver thread ran under a stone. She dug gently and found a hidden spring.

Now a small mouse guards her dreams.
