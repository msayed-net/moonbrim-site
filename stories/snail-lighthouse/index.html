The Snail Who Lit the Lighthouse

The night the great light died, the slowest snail on the cliff began to climb. A gentle stormy-night bedtime story about Pearl, whose glistening trail — the thing she always hid — becomes the light that brings three boats home. For every little one who feels too small and too slow.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

The night the great light died. At its foot lived Pearl, the slowest snail. Three small boats searched the waves for home.

On the quay, small lanterns waited for fathers. So Pearl began to climb, inch by inch. The wall climbed higher as the storm chased.

The rain came sideways, and still she climbed. A cold gust tore her from the wall. She stopped in the rain, small and late.

Then moonlight found her trail and glowed. Inch by inch, her nights work began shining. A silver spiral led the boats home.

And Pearl slept, a tiny lighthouse safe.
