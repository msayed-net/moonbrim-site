The Root Who Worked in the Dark

Everyone watched the little sprout. No one watched below. A gentle bedtime story about Root, who grew down in the dark while her twin drank the morning — and the burning July day when the invisible work saved everything. For every little one whose hard work goes unseen.

Everyone watched the sprout, not what grew below. Under the garden, her other half grew down. Root worked in the dark; Sprout drank sunlight.

The garden cheered for every new leaf above. Down below, the dark was quiet and deep. July burned hot, and rain forgot the garden.

Sprout bent low as the garden fell silent. So Root dug deeper, past worms and stones. She curled around the great blocking rock.

Deeper than any root had ever gone. There in the blackness, she touched hidden water. Cool silver climbed her up to the light.

The sprout stood tall, holding an invisible hand.
