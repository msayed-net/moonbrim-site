Bramble and the Baby Blanket Patches

The night Bramble tore, a shiny new bear arrived — and the old teddy decided to hide himself away with the forgotten toys. A gentle bedtime story about a very loved, very worn bear, and the grandmother who stitched him back together with pieces of a baby blanket. Because mended things carry the most love.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

The night Bramble tore, everyone went quiet. Seven winters of hugs wore him soft thin. Then a new bear arrived, shiny and cold.

That night, the new bear got the pillow. Bramble tucked himself together and hid the tear. That night the hug pulled the seam open.

So Bramble climbed away to forgotten toys. The chest closed, and the dark grew quiet. The old toys whispered we were loved too.

But morning came calling his name again. Grandma mended him with pieces of her blanket. His new arm hugged even better than before.

Now he wears her story in patches.
