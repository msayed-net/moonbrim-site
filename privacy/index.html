Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Moonbrim ("we," "us") makes bedtime story videos for children and families, published on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest. This page explains how this website, moonbrim.com, handles information.

The short version

This website collects no personal data. There are no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no sign-up forms, and no advertising on moonbrim.com. It is a simple, static page that links out to our social profiles.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technology on this site. We do not run analytics scripts, and we do not ask visitors to submit any personal information here. If you email us at hello@moonbrim.com, we will see your email address and whatever you choose to write to us — we use that only to reply to you, and we do not add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone else.

Third-party platforms

Our story videos are distributed on third-party platforms — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest. When you watch, follow, comment, or interact with Moonbrim content on those platforms, your data is handled under that platform's own privacy policy and terms, not this one. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of whichever platform you use.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Moonbrim's content is made for children, and we take that responsibility seriously. This website does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children — in fact, it does not collect personal information from anyone at all, by design. Our videos on YouTube are labeled "made for kids" in accordance with COPPA, and each platform's own child-directed protections apply to interactions that happen on that platform.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to notify you about beyond the words on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@moonbrim.com.
