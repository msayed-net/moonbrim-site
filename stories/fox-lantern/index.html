The Little Fox and the Lantern

A gentle bedtime story about Finn, a little fox who finds a magic lantern and learns that the brightest light of all is a kind heart.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Finn the fox woke up in his den. Finn loved exploring the golden autumn woods. One day he found a small golden lantern.

As night fell, Finn carried the lantern home. Fireflies gathered, and the forest felt like magic. Then Finn heard a lost bunny crying.

My lantern will light our way. Together they found the bunny family waiting. The brightest light is a kind heart.
