The Sock Who Lost His Twin

Two socks went into the washer. Only one came out. A gentle bedtime story about Blue, who searched the whole wilderness of the house for his missing twin — and what he found instead in the lost and found box. For every little one missing a friend who moved away.

This story premieres on YouTube soon — check back if the video isn't live yet.

Only one sock came back from washing. Blue and his twin always matched. They warmed two feet every winter.

Now the drawer felt empty and quiet. So Blue set out into the wilderness. The dust bunnies had seen nothing.

He searched the washer, a soapy cave. Behind the radiator, he found one thread. He held the thread all night, and understood.

The lost and found box was full. A polka dot sock was crying softly. They did not match, but fit perfectly.

Small feet wore them proudly, mismatched together.
