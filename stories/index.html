The Backpack Who Was Scared of School

The night before the first day of school, Mila finds her brand new backpack hiding under the bed — trembling. A gentle bedtime story about first-day fear, and how comforting someone else is secretly how we get brave ourselves. For little dreamers starting something new, and the grown-ups walking them there.
